R Sabee SMS Operational Alerts

R Sabee employees and authorized personnel may voluntarily enroll to receive operational text-message alerts relating to production equipment and maintenance.

Messages may include: machine-down alerts, machine-recovery alerts, PLC communication alerts, production-equipment status alerts, and maintenance notifications.

How to opt in

Provide your name and mobile number using the R Sabee enrollment process and affirmatively select the SMS consent checkbox. The SMS consent choice is not selected by default.

Important: This public page documents the consent language shown at enrollment. R Sabee only sends these operational messages to people who have affirmatively opted in through its enrollment process.
