R Sabee SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This policy applies to the R Sabee operational SMS notification program.

Information collected

R Sabee may collect a participant's name, mobile phone number, SMS consent status, and information needed to administer operational notifications.

How information is used

Mobile information is used to send operational messages such as machine-down alerts, machine-recovery alerts, PLC communication alerts, equipment-status alerts, and maintenance notifications, and to administer opt-in and opt-out requests.

Mobile information and consent are not shared for marketing

R Sabee does not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on equipment and plant conditions. Message and data rates may apply according to the participant's wireless plan.

Opting out and help

Participants may reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent

SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of employment. Consent to receive SMS messages is collected separately and affirmatively.
