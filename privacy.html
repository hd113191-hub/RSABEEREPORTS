R Sabee SMS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

R Sabee uses SMS messaging exclusively to notify authorized employees about production equipment status, maintenance alerts, PLC communication issues, and machine downtime.

Information We Collect

We collect employee mobile phone numbers only for operational maintenance notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • Machine down alerts
  • Machine running/recovery notifications
  • PLC communication failure alerts
  • Maintenance notifications

Sharing of Information

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on equipment conditions and maintenance events.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance or contact your supervisor.
