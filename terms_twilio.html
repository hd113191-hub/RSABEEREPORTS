R Sabee SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 3, 2026

These terms apply to the R Sabee operational SMS notification program.

Program description

Participants who voluntarily opt in may receive recurring operational text messages from R Sabee. Messages may include machine-down alerts, machine-recovery alerts, PLC communication alerts, production-equipment status alerts, and maintenance notifications.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on equipment and plant conditions.

Charges

Message and data rates may apply according to your wireless service plan. R Sabee does not charge a separate fee for participating in the SMS program.

Opt out

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. After an opt-out request is processed, no further program messages will be sent to that mobile number unless the participant later provides new consent.

Help

Reply HELP for assistance with the SMS program.

Consent

Participation is voluntary. SMS consent is not a condition of employment. Consent to receive SMS messages is collected separately from other agreements.

Privacy

R Sabee does not share, sell, rent, or otherwise provide mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
